The global fest

Qiskit Fall Fest

Qiskit Fall Fest is IBM Quantum’s worldwide series of student-run quantum computing events. Every year, campuses across the globe design and host their own version — from beginner workshops to full hackathons — using Qiskit, IBM’s open-source SDK for programming quantum circuits on simulators and real quantum hardware.

No prior quantum background is required to take part. If you can write a little Python, you can write a quantum circuit by the end of Day 1.

The host

DBATU, Lonere

Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar Technological University, Lonere is proud to bring Qiskit Fall Fest to Konkan for the first time. This edition is run by students, for students — built around the same workshop-then-hackathon format used at Fall Fest campuses worldwide, adapted for our own labs and timelines.

Whether you’re a first-year exploring quantum computing for the first time or a senior who already knows what a Hadamard gate does, there’s a track built for you.

Good to know

FAQ

Do I need a quantum computing background?

No. Day 1 workshops start from first principles — qubits, gates, and measurement — before anyone touches the hackathon problem statements.

What team size is allowed for the hackathon?

Teams of 2–4 for the General Track. The Freshers Track is individual.

Do I need my own quantum hardware?

No. Everything runs through Qiskit on simulators, with access to real IBM Quantum backends provided during the event.

Is there a registration fee?

No, participation is free for all DBATU students.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes — every participant who submits a project receives a certificate, with additional recognition for winners.
